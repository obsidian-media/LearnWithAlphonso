### Overall assessment of the source audit

The audit's main no-go conclusion is supported. F01, F03, and two F04 defects are independently reproduced. F02's separate read/compute/write sequence and checked-but-nonatomic parallel writes are directly visible. F05–F09 source/configuration concerns remain present. F10–F15 correctly describe evidence or coverage gaps; they should not be misreported as observed user incidents or proven misconfiguration. Build/CI/TestFlight successes are real but cannot cancel the reproduced defects.

The owner checklist's statement that only the Paid Apps Agreement remains is contradicted by the four reproductions and F02. The agreement was expressly excluded from the source audit's blocker count; keep that accounting convention, while recognizing that [Apple says paid IAP will not function in review until it is active](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/configure-in-app-purchases). Thus purchase proof has an external dependency. Apple also confirms sandbox testing does not require prior App Review approval and that the first subscription, its group, and app version must be in the same review submission ([sandbox guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/configure-in-app-purchases), [submission guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-submissions-to-app-review/submit-an-in-app-purchase/)).

## Execution order and dependencies

1. **Stabilize correctness and privacy (engineering).** Implement F01/F02/F03/F04 in separate reviewable changes with shared web/Deno contract tests. F01 and F02 require a real database test environment; F03 needs a bounded recovery policy; F04 needs an agreed safe export scope. Keep installed-client compatibility explicit. Then F05/F06/F08.
2. **Make the release reproducible (engineering/release).** Close F07/F11, deploy the required backend/migration changes through controlled gates, record a source/backend/migration manifest, and archive a new signed candidate if native code changed. Verify the exported IPA, including toolchain, identity, entitlements, privacy manifest, assets, endpoint and production public purchase key.
3. **Prove the exact candidate on a device and in ASC (release/account owner).** Complete F10/F13/F14 and S01–S10. Purchase tests depend on an active Paid Apps Agreement; that dependency is recorded without changing the source audit's explicit exclusion from its blocker count. Capture new screenshots/recording *after* the final UI/build is selected. Read back saved ASC state.
4. **Harden monitored rollout (product/operations).** Close F09/F12/F15, run a small controlled cohort, inspect sync/review/payment/speech/moderation metrics and support load, then expand only after signed acceptance. Core lessons should continue if Hector or social matching is disabled.

The release decision must be made from one exact candidate: native build and source SHA, deployed web/Edge SHA, migration watermark, course/content version, configuration evidence, approved ASC metadata and dated device test record. If any component changes after sign-off, rerun its affected checks. No calendar promise is justified without engineering design and physical-device/account access.

## Audit corrections and confidence boundaries

- The report is right to distinguish source defects from incidents: no production data-loss incident, purchase-code bug, privacy-label mismatch, infringement or backup failure was proven.
- TestFlight beta review is a different status from production App Store review. The observed pending external beta state does not satisfy S01–S10.
- The report's four failure injections were rerun successfully as *reproductions* here, but they are ignored local diagnostic files. They should become maintained regression tests as fixes land.
- `sharp` advisory severity does not itself establish iOS runtime reachability; the resolved version and tooling exposure are still worth fixing.
- The source report's broad history/secret scan and live legal-page check were not repeated in this verification. Their findings remain prior-audit evidence, not a fresh certification of all history or jurisdictions.
- [Apple account-deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion) calls for Sign in with Apple token revocation; it does not require indefinite retention of a user's entire account while Apple is unavailable. [Apple privacy guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) covers relevant third-party collection and requires accurate published answers.
