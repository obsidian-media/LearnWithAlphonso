Need help? Just saying "Help me!" sounds like an emergency. Here's the friendly way to ask.
With friends, "Can you" is perfect. "Can you pass the salt?"
With strangers or your boss, try "Could you." It's a little softer. "Could you help me with this?"
Want to be extra polite? "Would you mind helping me?" Notice: after "mind", we say "helping", not "help".
Quick test! You're asking your teacher. Which sounds more polite? A: "Can you explain that again?" Or B: "Could you explain that again, please?"
Both work, but B is softer!
